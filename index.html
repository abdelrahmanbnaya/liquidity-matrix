The method

Three things we look for on every chart

◆

Pools of stops

Equal highs, equal lows and clean swing points: the places where many traders put their stop loss — and where price is drawn to.

◆

Sweeps and walls

A wick through a level that closes back inside, or a zone price hits again and again without closing through. We show what happened next — including when it failed.

◆

Ranges and breaks

Where price compresses, where the ceiling and floor are, and whether the break really followed through on the candles after it.

We test ideas on years of data before we talk about them, and we publish the ones that fail too. Liquidity Matrix is a way to read the chart — not a signal service and not a promise of profit.
