LM Flow: a trend indicator that waits for the break

It follows the trend, marks the level that matters, and only flags a change of direction when price breaks that level and holds beyond it.

◆

1 · It follows the trend

Line under price: the trend is up. Line above price: the trend is down. It moves with price and does not try to call the top or the bottom.

◆

2 · It waits at the level

While price ranges, nothing changes. The level stays on the chart until price actually decides.

◆

3 · Break and hold

A touch is not enough. The change is flagged only after price breaks the level and holds beyond it.

What LM Flow is not

  • It is not a signal service and it does not tell you when to buy or sell.
  • It does not predict. It reacts to what price has already done, so it is late by design.
  • In sideways markets it can flip back and forth, and a break can fail.
  • No promise of results. One example on a past chart does not mean the next move will look the same.

Indicators help you read the chart. They don't make the decision. You do.
