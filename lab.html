THE LAB

The lab: what we tested, and what we found

Every trading idea we test runs on years of data and is compared with random entries that use the same stop, target, session and costs. If it does not clearly beat them, we write it here, even when it is our own idea.

Compared with luck

A thousand random traders per test, entering at random times under the same conditions as the idea.

Locked data

Part of the history stays closed until the very end, so an idea cannot be tailored to it.

Number of tries

The more ideas we try, the harder the bar, because luck alone makes good-looking results if you try enough.

What does this tell us?

Short chart patterns on their own did not beat luck after costs in our tests. That is why we offer the liquidity map as a tool to understand and learn the chart, not as an entry signal. When an idea passes the public test, it appears here first.
