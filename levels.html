How to read this page

What these numbers are — and are not

  • Times are in UTC. The page updates every morning and again at each session open (Asia, London, New York). Price keeps moving between updates, so check a live chart before acting on any level.
  • Two different kinds of level. The untouched highs and lows on each card are the nearest daily swing points price has not traded through. The session levels on each market page are the highs and lows of the Asia, London and New York sessions. They are separate lists: a session high can be taken while an untouched high above it is still waiting.
  • Estimates, not orders. A level marks where stops are likely to sit, based on the candles. Nobody outside the broker can see the actual orders. Price may reach a level, break straight through it, or never come back to it.
