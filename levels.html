Two scenarios to watch — not a prediction

Sweep above

Price runs the highs near 4199 and into the 4217–4226 zone. Watch whether it closes back below the zone (a sweep that fails to hold) or closes through it (a breakout that has to be respected).

Sweep below

Price runs the lows near 4126, then 4073. Watch whether it closes back above (stops taken, buyers return) or keeps closing below (the move continues).

These are levels where stops are likely to sit, not entries. Price can go through any of them. Always use a stop loss and keep risk small — a common rule is 1% of the account or less on a single trade.
