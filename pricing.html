Can I try before I pay?

Yes. Every service starts with a free sample on your market (for indicators and EAs: a free written spec and a fixed quote). You pay only if you want to continue.

How do I pay?

After you approve the sample we send the details and a payment link. Monthly plans can be cancelled any month.

Do you give buy/sell signals or promise results?

No. Everything here is education and tools built on real market data. We make no promise of profit, and the scenarios on our market pages show every outcome, losses included, on our public track record.

Do you need my trading password?

Never. For a journal review, a statement export or a read-only (investor) password is enough. For custom code we only need your rules.

We are opening these services now. Launch prices are kept for clients who start now, for as long as they stay.
