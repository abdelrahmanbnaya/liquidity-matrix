Privacy policy

This website does not use advertising cookies or sell any data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records basic technical information (such as IP addresses) in its server logs to keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and does not follow you to other sites; it gives us totals only, such as page views, countries, the sites visitors came from and the type of device.

Requests sent with the form are saved in our own Google Sheet (Google) and we get a notification on Telegram. If that service cannot be reached, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) forwards the request to our inbox instead. These services process what you type only to deliver it to us.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and, if you asked for it, to send you the market levels. You can ask us to stop or to delete your messages at any time by replying to any email or writing to jarvislmatrix@gmail.com.

Links to YouTube and other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
