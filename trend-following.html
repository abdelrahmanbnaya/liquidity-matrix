The rules (published before any result)

  1. Eight classic trend-following rules: breakouts of the 20, 55 and 100-day high or low (exits on 10, 20 and 50 days), the 20/100 and 50/200 moving-average crosses, and 3, 6 and 12-month momentum, checked once a week.
  2. Entry at the close of the day the signal appears.
  3. A fixed stop 3 times the average daily range (ATR 20) away.
  4. Exit by each rule's own exit, or at the stop.
  5. Results in R (profit divided by the risk) after an assumed typical spread. Overnight swap is not included, because it differs from broker to broker. No position sizes and no balances.

Why these rules?

Trend following is one of the most studied methods across many markets. In our historical tests (2018 to October 2026) it was the only one that beat random entries on gold.

It did not beat them on EUR/USD, nor on a group of 13 markets tested together over 2018–2022. So we do not treat it as proven: this public test will decide.

A historical test is not a promise. Past results do not guarantee future results, and trend trades lose more often than they win; they live on a few large winners.
